[2026-10-25 22:11] derbymanager : Condition issues: there are no issues. The game was showing you the injury chance, which is a combination of condition and form. But that was confusing so now it's showing condition only and exact injury chance is not visible.  Injury chance was a bit too high so I reduced it.  Changed how subs work a bit. You can set player's match status for any player (pitch or bench) like so:  Gets in: - RAT: Replaces a player in his position that has a rating 0.5 or more lower than the average of his team - CON: Replaces a player in his position that has condition below 80% - AUTO: Only gets in when he fits someone else's get out requirement (he is the right position) - DEV: Gets in only when the score is +/-2, combining rating and condition who to replace - IMP: Gets in as a priority if the team isn't winning - EMR: Only gets in on an injury  Gets out: - RAT: If he has a rating 0.5 or more lower than the average of his team or lower than 6.5 if anyone on the bench can replace him. - CON: If he has less than 80% condition if anyone on the bench can replace him - AUTO: combines rating and condition - DEV: Gets out as a prioirty when the team isn't winning. When winning he is treated as auto. - IMP: Can get out only if winning by two goals, in which case he is treated as auto. - EMR: Not a part of planned changes, only gets out on an injury  Yellow cards add a bit of weight to all of these. Subs always happen between 55-65 minutes (and a 3rd around 110 if it comes to that).  Player's training requirement behaves like this: - it goes down by 1 if he plays at least 20 minutes - it goes down by 2 if he played at least 45 minutes - it goes back to 1 no matter where it was if he played 75 minutes or more  Build up now warns you when there is an overflow of players in some area or when there are too few players in a critical area. Each warning reduces your build up.  And adjustments to the engine so defenders hold the line better, players don't tire so much from running, etc.
