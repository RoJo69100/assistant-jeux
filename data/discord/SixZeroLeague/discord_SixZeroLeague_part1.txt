[2026-10-04 17:06] gazellemag : **Six Zero League 0.5.7 and 0.5.8 update** **Part 1 of 2 | On the court**  The biggest update we have shipped, and most of it lands on the pitch.  **Matches** Fouls can now cost a defender: a yellow is a warning, a 2 minute exclusion sends him to the side and leaves his team one short, and a red card ends his night. A red card with a referee's report also bans him for two league rounds. Hard challenges can put a player down: play stops, the medics come on, and your substitute for his role takes his place. When the bench runs dry a side plays short, and it shows at both ends of the court. Goalkeepers now wear their own kit, and the ball no longer jumps across the court.  **Players and careers** Minutes on court are recorded every match and feed the training week, so the players you actually use improve faster. Role training lets you teach a player a second and third position, and a learned role stays with him if you sell him. Age catches up in the off season: players age a year when the season ends, and from 33 they can announce retirement and play one final season. Every club also carries a rating now, from Grassroots to Legendary.  **Tactics** You can now set your own 6-0 defence: switch the Tactics tab to Defence and drag your six outfielders between the spots you want them in. Naming a substitute for each role happens on the same screen. Injured and suspended players are clearly marked wherever you see them.

The transfer market has been rebuilt with live updates and player profiles.  **Clubs** Your badge can have a shape. Player profiles now show your club crest and the shirt number on a jersey, and the header no longer scrolls away on a phone. Live matches also use about half the data they did before.
[2026-10-09 16:17] gazellemag : **Six Zero League 0.5.9 update:**  - Overall UI/UX consolidation - The Overview is redesigned: next match, last result and next report side by side, with this week's events and the league table below. - Player profiles have Attributes and Statistics tabs, with this season's and all-time career statistics. - Player statistics are private to their club unless the player is listed on the transfer market. - The full-time report has player-of-the-match cards with role badges, a cleaner scorer list, and fits without scrolling. - Match cards show the win, draw or loss result, with the score centered. - Technical fouls now match what the player was doing: travelling is the most common, charging needs a defender in front, and double dribbles are rare. - After a passive-play warning, an attack has six passes to get a shot away. - The injury rate is back to its intended level. - League matches interrupted by a server restart go back to pending instead of getting stuck. - Every column of the Team view can be sorted.  - Infrastructure cards are simpler and show what the next level unlocks and how long an upgrade takes to pay back. - On phones, the menu opens under its button as a simple list. - The wiki now covers technical fouls, passive play, the live viewer, player statistics, role training and infrastructure. - **Club:**    - A new Club page brings together Finances, Infrastructure and your club's Identity (name, colours and crest), and sits second in the menu.    - Infrastructure cards show what the next level brings, what it unlocks and how long an upgrade takes to pay back.    - Crests have 100 icons and 50 shapes to choose from. - A commentary bar in the clubs' colours narrates each attack, with goals flashing in the scorer's kit. - The live viewer has Incidents and Stats tabs below the court. - League points are now 2 for a win, 1 for a draw and 0 for a loss, and ties are decided by goal difference, then head-to-head, then goals scored.
[2026-10-16 15:03] gazellemag : **Six Zero League 0.6.0 update - League divisions & Cup competition**  **Divisions** - Clubs now play across five divisions. Every new club starts in the 5th. - Each league champion goes up a division; the bottom three go down. - Higher divisions earn more from tickets, sponsors, facilities and prizes.  **Cup** - A new knockout cup runs alongside the league every season, for every manager-run club. - Ties are single matches at a neutral venue. The best-placed clubs skip the early rounds. - A level tie goes to extra time, then a 7 metre shootout played out on court. - Every cup tie pays a share of the gate and counts toward your players' match development. - The winner takes the cup prize and the Alpha Cup Winner trophy.  **Competitions** League and Cup tabs, with your matches from both in playing order. The Cup tab has the full bracket. Live ties show their scores and can be watched. The league picker groups leagues under their division.  **Injuries and suspensions** - Injuries last 1 to 6 matches, most of them short. - High intensity training now carries more risk and longer injuries, so a deeper squad pays off. - An injury or ban that outlasts the season carries over into the next one.  **Match engine** - Extra time and shootouts have their own commentary lines. Extra-time goals show as 61' to 70'. - The 7 metre shootout is played at a realistic pace. Takers walk up and stand set on the line before they shoot, and the keepers wait beside the goal. - 6 and 9 metre shots are now proper jump shots. The shooter runs straight into his jump from the line, releases in the air and lands inside. - Line violations are only called when the shooter takes off from the line. - After a line violation, the ball travels to the keeper instead of jumping into his hands. - Teams on a passive play warning now push for a shot instead of passing the limit away, so passive play is called far less often. - Fixed a rare bug where a keeper could keep the ball until half-time.
[2026-10-24 13:26] gazellemag : **Six Zero League 0.6.1 update: Tactical Depth**  **New** - Tactics: choose an attack formation (5-1 or 4-2), a defense formation (6-0, 5-1, 3-2-1 or 3-3), an attack direction and an aggression level (Light, Normal, Hard). - Match orders: plan up to three tactical changes that kick in at a minute and score you choose. - Tactics report: the pros and cons of each choice, checked against your own players. - Scouting: both teams' tactics show in the match stats and report, and your Next match card shows what your opponent played last time. **Improvements** - Starters and substitutes are now highlighted in the tactics player list. - On large screens, clicking a player on the tactics court opens his profile. - Competitions view better navigation. - Cup match reports now say when a tie was decided after extra time or on penalties. - Recent events show the right round for each competition, including cup round names. - Finances: the tickets estimate shows how many home games are played and how many are still to come. - Transfer market: a player's profile now shows the physical points he lost to age last season. **Gameplay and balance** - Passive play: after the warning a team has 4 passes, and the pass that takes a free throw doesn't count. - Fewer 2-minute exclusions, and they no longer pile up in bursts. - A team with an extra player makes better use of it. - Fewer injuries: the training injury chance is halved, with the three intensities balanced against each other. - Defenders now mark pivots properly, including against two pivots. - Attackers keep a better distance from pressing defenses. - The formations are balanced against each other, and the 4-2 is now a real alternative to the 5-1. **Fixes** - Cup ties: extra time is always played in full (no golden goal), and only a tie still level after it goes to a shootout. - Fouls, penalties and 2-minute exclusions are only given to a defender actually in contact with the attacker.
