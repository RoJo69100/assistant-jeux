data/discord/DerbyManager/discord_DerbyManager_part1.txt:36 | [2024-10-01 16:16] derbymanager : **Updates to training** The idea is that a few players that are just right for your team grow faster than the others, while majority grows slowly or stagnates. I wanted to avoid all young players predictably growing and the game turning into farming simulator... So how does it work? It's quite complex as I wanted potential, club fit, playtime, age, facilities, mentors, and, of course, random to have a meaningful impact. I also wanted to hide all of that from us so that we don't have to worry about it 🙂  So, there are two things to observe with training: - The first and the more important one is how well the player develops. Players with good club fit **or** potential will grow faster. Good growth might mean they have low potential but great club fit or vice versa. Club fit is randomly generated whenever a player changes team and can be modified between seasons (and rarely during season). Potential is predefined for every player. Of course, amount of playtime dictates growth too and without it the player simply won't grow (except in school).  - Icons on the training screen will tell you how well your facilities work for your players. Some players might find the intensity too low, others too high, and some might simply be too good for your club. Changing intensity (selecting different skill to train from technical and tactical and turning mentors on or off) can help you put focus on your best growing players. Your goal should be to make a setup as good as possible for your best-growing players, which might mean sacrificing others that don't train well and leaving them with yellow icons.  In simplest terms, to gain the most out of training, give the most playtime to players that have the highest growth and, as a secondary and less important objective, set icons to favour those players.
