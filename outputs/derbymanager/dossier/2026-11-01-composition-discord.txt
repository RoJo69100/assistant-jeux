data/discord/DerbyManager/discord_DerbyManager_part3.txt:570 | [2023-02-26 14:07] derbymanager : I did training just recently and here's how it works. Training will be calculated directly after the match is played. That means a player can only grow when he plays a game and for the position he played in. During the night update stats might drop for those who didn't play the game.  Players accumulate "training points" every day which increases their chance of getting a skill increase. When skill increase does happen, they lose appropriate amount of training points. Mechanically that just means that if the player doesn't get a skill increase today - he is more likely to get it tomorrow. But also, when he does get it, he is less likely to get it again the next day.  You'll be able to set up one of 4 trainings for the whole squad: - Physical: Improves work rate, speed and strength. These grow faster for younger players and drop faster for older ones. - Tactical: Improves marking, off the ball, positioning, and focus. These all change at moderate rate. - Mental: Improves passing, finishing, awareness, and determination. Mental skills change fast except for determination that is hard to change through training (depends on other things). - Technical: Improves handling, tackling, accuracy, technique. These grow slow but rarely drop.  There is also fitness skill that can drop due to injury and will grow first if it's too low. For example, a player at low fitness will first have to increase fitness to some normal level before he gets his other skills improved.  Determination, focus and work rate are skills that together cover all areas of play. They are used on top of any skill check in the match engine as a bonus, so keeping them high will be important. So the choice for training is to find the one that fits the best for what your team needs at the moment, but also to rotate players enough to get the most out of them, but also to balance between the skills they need and whichever one of these 3 (determination, focus and work rate) is too low for the whole team.
