data/discord/DerbyManager/discord_DerbyManager_part4.txt:2198 | [2025-07-06 20:22] derbymanager : So, a quick recap of finances, again:  - Stadium revenue and match rewards are not included in daily finances as they're variable of course. You might play a home game and a cup game in one day and earn a lot, and then the next two days you can play away and earn 0. - Instead, the board uses an estimated daily stadium revenue. So, they expect you will earn 1M from today's home game, then 0 from tomorrow's away game, and so on for the whole season. Average is 500k per day and they use that for their calculations. - Based on that they will invest into facilities as much as they can without going too far into negative daily money. They also won't invest into facilities if they estimate your facilities are too high for the league.  So why are my facilities downgrading?  Because you don't have enough money to invest into them. Let's say you just sold a player and have 5M on your account. The board is downgrading your facilities by 200k per day to keep those 5M intact so you can buy players. They could, instead, keep your facilities at 100% and they wouldn't downgrade, but you would lose 200k per day and in less than a season (25 days to be exact) you would lose those 5M and couldn't improve your squad.  Since your facilities downgrade for the same price it costs to improve them you can just buy back the level you lost for cash, assuming you have cash to do so. Financially and mathematically it is exactly the same. There is no benefit if the board is investing money for you or if you do it manually yourself. It's just moving money from one bucket into another.  Hope that helps.
